Fiona Young

I received my Ph.D. from Cornell University in 2024 in pure mathematics, specializing in combinatorics and discrete geometry. Prior to Cornell, I studied mathematics at Harvard University. Throughout my career, I have worked at the intersection of mathematics, technology, and education. I have led international educational initiatives, launched and scaled STEM programs, trained educators, and built learning communities spanning thousands of students. Across these experiences, I have been motivated by a belief that talent is far more widely distributed than opportunity. The most important ideas in mathematics and technology should not remain accessible only to specialists. My goal is to help more people develop the knowledge, confidence, and curiosity needed to engage with them. Whether through teaching, program leadership, or technical innovation, I strive to make powerful ideas more accessible and more impactful.

Each one of us has one precious life. In the time we walk on this earth together, I hope to share with as many people as possible the gift of mathematical beauty :-)
