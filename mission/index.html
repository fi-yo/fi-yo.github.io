Mission

On human potential

My work as a mathematician and educational leader has always been driven by a deep fascination with how people learn. Building educational programs over the years has shown me firsthand that human potential is far more widely distributed than opportunity. For most of history, a person's intellectual path depended heavily on the luck of geography and the chance of finding the right mentor.

Artificial intelligence is dismantling those historical barriers. We are heading toward a reality where anyone with curiosity can access tools that provide immediate feedback and foster genuine understanding. AI is reshaping who gets to learn, what they can learn, and how quickly they can transform curiosity into capability.

Gathering knowledge is just the starting point. The real purpose of learning is to build the independence required to shape the world around us. When AI takes over the mechanics of explaining concepts and providing feedback, it clarifies the irreplaceable role of educators in offering mentorship and cultivating ambition. Technology acts as a lever that helps people solve difficult problems and uncover talents they never realized they possessed.

Bringing that future to life is the core of my professional mission. I want to help build a landscape where powerful ideas are truly accessible to everyone, ensuring a person's trajectory is limited only by how far their imagination will take them.
